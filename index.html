About Barangay 28

Barangay 28 is an urban barangay in Caloocan City, NCR.

Barangay 28 is located in Caloocan City, NCR. It is a densely populated urban barangay with residential areas, small businesses, and active community programs focused on cleanliness, safety, and proper waste management.

The barangay promotes segregation of waste, recycling initiatives, and regular clean-up drives to maintain a healthy environment.
